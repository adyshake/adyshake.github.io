Why am I blogging?

2016-09-03

Teaching someone a particular concept always reinforced my learning, so I figured writing down technical posts would be a great resource to both whoever might enjoy reading my melodramatic writing and for myself, to serve as a reference in case I forget it later. It's a bit daunting though, writing about something you feel you haven't mastered, i.e. telling someone "How you should do X". See impostor syndrome

I'd like to think of this process as documentation of my learning process (filled with sweet sweet gifs)
